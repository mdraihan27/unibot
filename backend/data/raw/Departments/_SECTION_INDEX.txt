KNOWN DATA-GENERATION LIMITATIONS (BE AWARE FOR RAG TESTING)
================================================================
1. NAME COLLISIONS: With ~230 total synthetic faculty names generated
   from a deliberately small, deterministic name pool, a small number
   (~14) of full name collisions exist across DIFFERENT departments
   (e.g., two unrelated departments each happening to have a "Prof.
   Dr. Salma Ahmed"). This is a known artifact of small pool size,
   not a data integrity bug — no single department repeats a name
   internally, and no Chairman name repeats across departments.

2. ROSTER COMPLETENESS: Faculty rosters are explicitly marked
   "SAMPLE" in each file — they represent 4 to 9 synthetic entries
   per department, not necessarily a complete department faculty
   list. A real department may have more (or fewer) actual faculty
   members than shown here.

3. DVM PROGRAM STRUCTURE: The 10 Veterinary Medicine departments
   collectively deliver ONE degree program (DVM, 5 years including
   internship), unlike other faculties where each department
   delivers its own distinct degree. Only
   dept_vet_medicine_surgery_theriogenology.txt is marked as the
   "Major Department"; the other 9 are marked "Contributing
   Department." A RAG system should understand these 10 files
   describe ONE academic program taught across 10 departments, not
   10 separate degree programs.
