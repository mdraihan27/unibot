TRANSFER CREDIT AND WAIVER
==========================

1. TRANSFER FROM OTHER UNIVERSITIES (clause 8.2)
   - A student transferring from another recognized university
     applies to the Registrar (Section A) with transcripts and
     course syllabi.
   - The departmental credit evaluation committee (Chairman +
     two senior faculty) maps each completed course to a JUST
     course; credit is granted where content overlap is at
     least 80% and the grade earned is C or better (grade
     points do NOT transfer; transferred courses appear as
     "T" on the transcript).
   - Maximum transferable credit: 50% of total programme
     credits.
   - The student enters at the semester determined by the
     credited load and must complete at least 50% of the
     programme credits at JUST to earn the JUST degree.

2. INTER-JUST PROGRAMME TRANSFER
   - Current JUST students may apply for inter-departmental
     transfer after two semesters with CGPA 3.00 or above, in
     the annual window published by the Registrar.
   - Transfer depends on seat availability in the target
     department; credited courses apply per the equivalence
     list of the target department.

3. COURSE WAIVER (EXEMPTION WITHOUT CREDIT)
   Where content is similar but below the 80% threshold, the
   department may WAIVE a prerequisite without awarding credit;
   the student must then complete replacement credits to reach
   the programme total (degree_requirements.txt).

4. APPEAL
   Credit evaluation decisions may be appealed to the Dean
   within 10 working days; the Dean's decision is final.

5. INTERNATIONAL STUDENTS
   Transfers and credit evaluation for international students
   are coordinated with the International Service Centre
   (Section A); equivalence follows the same 80% rule with
   certified English translations of documents.
