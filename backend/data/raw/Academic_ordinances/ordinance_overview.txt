ACADEMIC ORDINANCE — OVERVIEW AND LEGAL BASIS
=============================================

1. LEGAL BASIS
   The academic ordinances of Jashore University of Science and
   Technology (JUST) derive their authority from the
   University Act and Statutes (see Section A,
   university_act_and_statutes_summary.txt). No academic rule
   in this section may contradict the University Act; where a
   conflict exists, the Act prevails.

   The principal ordinance governing undergraduate study is
   the "Academic Ordinance for the Undergraduate Programmes"
   (the "Ordinance"), administered by the Registrar's Office
   (Section A) and the Office of the Controller of
   Examinations (Section A), under the authority of the
   Academic Council.

2. STRUCTURE OF THE ORDINANCE
   The Ordinance is organized into ten numbered sections:
     Section 1  General provisions, definitions, applicability
     Section 2  Admission requirements and procedures
     Section 3  Semester system, credit hours, academic year
     Section 4  Course registration and enrolment
     Section 5  Grading and evaluation
     Section 6  Examinations and assessment
     Section 7  Academic standing: probation, suspension,
                termination, leave, readmission
     Section 8  Transfer credit and waiver
     Section 9  Thesis, project and internship
     Section 10 Degree requirements and award

   Each section contains numbered clauses (e.g., 3.4.1) and
   lettered sub-clauses (e.g., 3.4.1(a)). When citing a rule,
   ALWAYS cite the full clause number; partial citations are
   ambiguous because sub-clause (a) of clause 3.4.1 and
   clause 3.4.1(a) read as identical shorthand but refer to
   different nesting levels in some legacy documents.

3. AMENDMENT HISTORY
   - Original ordinance: adopted on foundation of the
     university; applied to the first undergraduate sessions.
   - Amendment I: grade-table revision (percentile letter
     grade table replaced by the current fixed-scale table).
   - Amendment II — "II Examination System, Effective from
     Session 2015-16": replaced the original examination
     structure (single final examination per course) with the
     current incourse + final examination system, revised the
     marks distribution, and introduced the supplementary
     examination provisions in supplementary_exams.txt.
     Status: CURRENT.
   - Archive: the pre-2015-16 examination system is retained
     for records of students who enrolled before 2015-16 and
     for archival completeness ONLY. It does NOT apply to any
     current or future student.

4. HOW TO USE THIS SECTION (FOR HUMANS AND FOR RAG RETRIEVAL)
   - Every rule file in this section is self-contained but
     cross-referenced; the index (_SECTION_INDEX.txt) maps
     topics to files.
   - Where a file in this section references "the Ordinance",
     it means the clause set summarized in that file, not a
     separate document.
   - Rules in this section apply to ALL undergraduate
     students of all faculties unless a file explicitly
     scopes an exception (e.g., DVM programme rules).
   - Faculty-specific or programme-specific deviations
     (e.g., DVM credit totals) are stated in the relevant
     rule file with the programme named.

5. INTERPRETATION AND APPEALS
   - Interpretation of the Ordinance rests with the Academic
     Council, on advice of the Registrar.
   - A student may appeal an academic decision (grade,
     attendance bar, malpractice finding, standing decision)
     per the appeals route stated in the relevant rule file
     in this section.
