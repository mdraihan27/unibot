GRADING POLICY — LETTER GRADES AND GRADE POINTS
===============================================

1. GRADE TABLE (clause 5.3, current — Amendment I scale)
   Letter Grade   Numerical Range (%)   Grade Point
   -------------------------------------------------
   A+             80 and above          4.00
   A              75–79                 3.75
   A-             70–74                 3.50
   B+             65–69                 3.25
   B              60–64                 3.00
   B-             55–59                 2.75
   C+             50–54                 2.50
   C              45–49                 2.25
   D              40–44                 2.00
   F              Below 40              0.00
   -------------------------------------------------
   - "D" is the minimum passing grade for theory courses in
     most programmes; some programmes (e.g., DVM, Pharmacy)
     require "C" or better in professional courses per their
     approved curriculum (Section G).
   - Laboratory courses commonly require a minimum of "C".

2. SPECIAL GRADES
   Grade   Meaning
   ----------------------------------------------------------
   I       Incomplete — granted only for documented illness
           or emergency in the final examination; must be
           cleared within one regular semester by sitting the
           supplementary/makeup examination; otherwise
           converts to F automatically.
   W       Withdrawn — see course_registration.txt §4.
   AU      Audit — no grade points.
   P/NP    Pass/No Pass — used for orientation, viva-voce and
           non-credit requirements where adopted; not counted
           in CGPA.
   X       Absent in final examination without valid reason —
           grade point 0.00, treated as F for standing.

3. GRADE CHANGES
   - A grade may be changed only for documented clerical or
     tabulation error, and only by the Controller of
     Examinations with the concurrence of the course teacher
     and the Chairman, within 30 days of result publication.
   - Students request re-totaling per result_publication.txt;
     grade changes never result from negotiation with the
     course teacher.

4. WITHHOLDING GRADES
   The Controller of Examinations may withhold a student's
   grades for unpaid dues (Section I) or a pending
   disciplinary case (Section J); grades are released when
   the hold clears.

5. TRANSCRIPT DISPLAY
   The transcript (procedures in Section H) shows, per
   course: code, title, credits, letter grade, grade points;
   and per semester: semester GPA (SGPA) and cumulative GPA
   (CGPA) per cgpa_computation.txt.
