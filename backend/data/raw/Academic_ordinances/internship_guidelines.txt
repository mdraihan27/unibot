INTERNSHIP / INDUSTRIAL ATTACHMENT GUIDELINES
=============================================

1. REQUIREMENT
   Programmes per their approved curriculum (Section G) require
   a supervised internship of 3–4 credits, normally in the
   break after Level 3 or early in the final year.

2. APPROVAL
   - The student submits the placement proposal (organization,
     duration, supervisor, work plan) to the departmental
     internship coordinator for approval before starting.
   - The department maintains a list of approved organizations;
   - self-arranged placements outside the list need additional
     approval.

3. DURATION AND CONDITIONS
   - Standard duration: 6–8 weeks full-time (or the equivalent
     part-time schedule approved by the coordinator).
   - The student remains enrolled and covered by university
     rules during the internship; workplace conduct reflects
     on the university (Section J applies).
   - No internship may be done in a close relative's direct
     supervision; conflicts of interest must be declared.

4. ASSESSMENT (100 marks)
   Component                          Marks
   ------------------------------------------------------------
   Organization supervisor's report   30
   Internship report                  30
   Presentation / viva                30
   Departmental coordinator's file    10
   ------------------------------------------------------------
   A failed internship is repeated in the next window;
   degree award requires a pass (degree_requirements.txt).

5. REPORT FORMAT
   Standard format issued by the department: title page,
   organization profile, work log, technical content, outcomes,
   supervisor's evaluation form, and the coordinator's
   sign-off page.

6. LOGISTICS AND SAFETY
   Travel and subsistence are the student's responsibility
   unless a placement provides support; the Transport Office
   (Section A) may assist with group travel to industrial
   sites. Safety incidents are reported to the Proctor's
   office (Section A) and the department.
