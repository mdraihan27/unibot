COURSE REGISTRATION AND ENROLMENT
=================================

1. REGISTRATION WINDOWS (clause 4.2)
   - Initial registration: during the first two weeks of a
     semester, per the academic calendar (Section K).
   - Late registration: weeks 3–4, with a late fee (Section I).
   - No registration is permitted after the end of week 4
     except by special permission of the Dean for documented
     reasons.

2. REGISTRATION PROCEDURE
   a. The student logs into the student portal and selects
      courses offered for the semester per the approved
      curriculum (Section G).
   b. The system checks prerequisites (course_numbering
      system.txt §3) and section capacity.
   c. The student pays the semester fee (Section I); the
      registration becomes ACTIVE only after payment
      confirmation by the Accounts Office (Section A).
   d. The student prints the registration slip and obtains
      the academic adviser's signature within week 2.

3. ADVISING
   Every student is assigned an academic adviser from the
   department faculty (rosters in Section D). The adviser:
   - Approves the course selection and credit load
   - Signs the registration slip
   - Monitors the advisee's CGPA and flags probation risk
     (probation_suspension_termination.txt)
   Advisers hold office hours posted on department notice
   boards and websites.

4. ADD / DROP
   - Add: within weeks 1–2 only (same window as late
     registration above).
   - Drop: within weeks 1–3, a course may be dropped without
     any record on the transcript.
   - Withdrawal (W): after week 3 through week 8, a course
     may be withdrawn with a "W" grade on the transcript.
     "W" carries no grade points and does not affect CGPA.
   - After week 8, withdrawal is not permitted except for
     approved medical/leave cases (leave_of_absence.txt),
     processed before the final examination starts.

5. SECTION ASSIGNMENT
   Sections are assigned by the department at registration.
   A student may not attend a section other than the assigned
   one without the Chairman's written permission. Capacity
   limits are enforced by the department; the Controller of
   Examinations (Section A) enforces them at examination
   seating.

6. REGISTRATION HOLDS
   A registration hold (cannot register for the next semester)
   is placed when:
   - Semester fee is unpaid (Section I)
   - Library books are overdue (Section A — Office of the
     Librarian)
   - Disciplinary case is pending (Section J)
   Holds are released by the responsible office, not by the
   department.

7. DUPLICATE REGISTRATION
   A student may not register for the same course twice in
   the same semester; a repeated course (improvement) is
   registered only after the earlier grade is posted.
