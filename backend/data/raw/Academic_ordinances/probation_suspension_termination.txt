ACADEMIC STANDING — PROBATION, SUSPENSION, TERMINATION
======================================================

1. GOOD STANDING
   A student is in good standing with SGPA/CGPA of 2.00 or
   above in the most recent semester.

2. PROBATION (clause 7.2)
   - Trigger: SGPA below 2.00 in any semester.
   - Effect: the student is placed on academic probation for
     one semester and must meet the adviser (Section D
     rosters) before registration; course load may be
     restricted by the Chairman (typically 12–15 credits).
   - Removal: SGPA of 2.00 or above in the probation
     semester.
   - Second consecutive probation semester → suspension
     (see §3).

3. SUSPENSION
   - Trigger: SGPA below 2.00 in two consecutive semesters.
   - Effect: suspension from the programme for one semester;
     the student may apply for readmission for the following
     semester with the Chairman's and Dean's approval.
   - During suspension the student may sit supplementary
     examinations (supplementary_exams.txt) to clear F
     grades.

4. TERMINATION (clause 7.4)
   - Trigger: any of the following —
       a. SGPA below 2.00 in three consecutive semesters;
       b. failure to clear a required course after three
          attempts;
       c. maximum duration exceeded (see §5);
       d. disciplinary expulsion (Section J).
   - Effect: termination from the programme; written notice
     issued by the Registrar's Office (Section A).
   - Reinstatement after termination is by appeal to the
     Academic Council only, within 30 days of the notice.

5. MAXIMUM PROGRAMME DURATION
   - 4-year programmes: maximum 6 years (12 regular semesters
     equivalent) from first enrolment.
   - 5-year DVM: maximum 7 years.
   - Extensions for documented medical/leave grounds are
     granted by the Academic Council on the Dean's
     recommendation (leave_of_absence.txt).

6. APPEALS
   Standing decisions (probation/suspension/termination) may
   be appealed in writing to the Chairman within 10 working
   days, then to the Dean, then to the Academic Council. The
   Academic Council's decision is final within the
     university.

7. NOTIFICATION
   Probation/suspension/termination notices are emailed to the
   student and posted to the guardian address on record; the
   Proctor's office (Section A) is copied for suspended
   students.
