ATTENDANCE POLICY
=================

1. MINIMUM ATTENDANCE (clause 6.5)
   A student must attend at least 75% of the classes (lecture
   and laboratory counted separately) in a course to be
   eligible to sit the final examination of that course.

2. ATTENDANCE RECORD
   - Teachers maintain daily attendance records and enter them
     in the student portal; students can view their running
     percentage throughout the semester.
   - The department publishes a provisional ineligible list
     one week before the final examination starts.
   - Objections must be filed with the Chairman within 3
     working days of publication.

3. CONSEQUENCES OF FALLING BELOW 75%
   a. The student is BARRED from the final examination and
      receives an "F" for the course (or "X" if absent without
      cause).
   b. The barred course must be repeated in full (registration,
      attendance, incourse, and final) — there is no
      attendance-only makeup.
   c. If the barred course is a prerequisite, the student
      cannot register for dependent courses until it is
      cleared (see course_registration.txt §3).

4. MEDICAL ABSENCES
   Absences due to illness are excused only with a medical
   certificate from the University Medical Centre (Section A
   — Chief Medical Officer) filed within one week of return.
   Excused absences still count against the 75% calculation
   unless the Medical Centre certifies a period of bed rest,
   in which case the 75% threshold is applied pro-rata to the
   attended period (clause 6.5(b)).

5. REPRESENTATIVE / CO-CURRICULAR DUTIES
   Attendance credit may be granted for university-sanctioned
   representation (sports, debate, NSS-type activities) with
   the prior written approval of the Chairman and Proctor
   (Section A); the approved list is circulated to teachers.

6. LABORATORY ATTENDANCE
   Lab attendance is counted separately. Missing a lab session
   requires a makeup within two weeks arranged with the lab
   instructor; otherwise the session counts as absent for the
   75% computation.
