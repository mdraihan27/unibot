ADMISSION REQUIREMENTS
======================

1. GENERAL ELIGIBILITY (clause 2.2)
   a. The applicant must have passed SSC/equivalent and
      HSC/equivalent examinations, or their recognized
      equivalents, in the science group (or the group
      specified for the target programme).
   b. Minimum GPA requirements (per the admission notice for
      the session; figures below are the synthetic dataset's
      standing defaults):
        - SSC (or equivalent): GPA 4.00 (out of 5.00)
        - HSC (or equivalent): GPA 4.00 (out of 5.00)
        - Combined (SSC+HSC): GPA 8.00
      Programmes with higher demand (CSE, Pharmacy, DVM) may
      set higher cutoffs in the annual admission notice.
   c. No more than one year gap between SSC and HSC is
      penalized; longer gaps are handled per admission
      notice rules.
   d. The applicant must satisfy any programme-specific
      subject requirements (e.g., Mathematics for
      Engineering programmes; Biology for Pharmacy, DVM,
      Microbiology).

2. ADMISSION ROUTES
   Route             Basis
   ----------------------------------------------------------
   Admission test    Central admission test administered per
                     the admission notice (Section K for dates)
   Quota             Freedom fighter, tribal, district, and
                     other quotas per University Act and the
                     admission notice
   Foreign/transfer  See transfer_credit.txt for credit
                     transfer; foreign admission through
                     International Service Centre (Section A)

3. SELECTION PROCESS
   a. Applications are submitted through the online admission
      portal in the window published in the admission notice.
   b. Eligible applicants sit the admission test; the merit
      list combines SSC+HSC GPA and test score per the
      formula in the admission notice.
   c. Selected candidates must complete admission formalities
      (verification, medical fitness, enrolment fee) by the
      published deadline. Failure to complete formalities
      forfeits the seat.

4. ENROLMENT
   After admission, the student is enrolled in Level-1,
   Semester-1 of the assigned programme and is issued a
   registration number and student ID card. The student ID
   card is valid for the duration of the programme and must
   be presented at examinations (examination_conduct.txt).

5. ADMISSION OF IRREGULAR STUDENTS
   Students admitted mid-programme from other universities
   follow transfer_credit.txt and register from the semester
   determined by the credit evaluation committee.

6. DISQUALIFICATION
   Admission is void if any document is found forged or
   misrepresented at any point; the matter is referred to the
   disciplinary authority (Section J).
