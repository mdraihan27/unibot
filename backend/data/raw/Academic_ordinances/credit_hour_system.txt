CREDIT HOUR SYSTEM
==================

1. DEFINITION OF ONE CREDIT HOUR (clause 3.4.1)
   One credit hour = one contact hour of instruction per week
   for one semester, where:
   - LECTURE course: 1 credit = 1 hour/week of lecture.
   - LABORATORY course: 1 credit = 3 hours/week of laboratory
     work (including sessional evaluation).
   - SESSIONAL/PROJECT work: credit assigned per approved
     curriculum; typically counted as laboratory-equivalent.

2. CONTACT HOUR WEEKLY PLAN
   A 3-credit lecture course with a 1-credit lab would meet:
     3 lecture hours + 3 laboratory hours per week.

3. TYPES OF COURSES AND CREDIT WEIGHTS
   Type                    Credits  Nature
   ------------------------------------------------------------
   Theory (lecture)        per code Lectures, midterms, final
   Laboratory              per code 3-hour weekly lab sessions
   Sessional/design        per code Studio/design evaluation
   Thesis/Project          4–6      Final-year capstone
   Internship              3–4      Supervised placement
   Viva-voce               0–1      Attached to thesis/project

4. TOTAL PROGRAMME CREDITS (undergraduate)
   Programme                          Total Credits
   ------------------------------------------------------------
   B.Sc. Engg. (4-year)               160
   B.Sc. (Hons) / B.Pharm / similar   150–160 (per approved
                                      curriculum; see Section G)
   BBA                                130–140
   B.A. (Hons) English                130
   DVM (Doctor of Veterinary Medicine) 5-year, 200+ per
                                      approved curriculum —
                                      governed by its own DVM
                                      schedule within the
                                      Ordinance
   Specific per-department totals and the 8-semester plan are
   in Section G (course catalog) and the department files
   (Section D).

5. HALF-CREDIT COURSES
   Not normally offered. All courses carry whole credit hours
   (1, 2, 3, or 4) except viva-voce (which may carry 0 credit
   as a required non-credit assessment).

6. AUDIT (NON-CREDIT) ENROLMENT
   A student may audit a course with the Chairman's
   permission. An audited course appears on the transcript
   with the grade "AU" (no grade points, not counted in CGPA).
   The auditor must meet the same attendance requirement
   (attendance_policy.txt) unless the Chairman grants written
   exemption.
