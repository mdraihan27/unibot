RESULT TABULATION, MODERATION AND PUBLICATION
=============================================

1. WHO DOES WHAT
   - Course teachers submit marks (incourse + final) to the
     department within 7 days of the examination.
   - The department compiles and forwards to the Controller of
     Examinations (Section A).
   - The Controller's office tabulates, applies moderation,
     computes SGPA/CGPA (cgpa_computation.txt), and publishes.

2. MODERATION (clause 5.6)
   - A departmental moderation committee (Chairman + two senior
     teachers) reviews the grade distribution per course.
   - If the failure rate in a course exceeds 40%, the
     committee must justify the distribution in writing; an
     automatic scaling UP of marks is not permitted, but a
     review examination may be recommended in exceptional,
     documented cases.
   - Moderation cannot change any individual student's marks
     by more than 5% and never across a grade boundary
     without the course teacher's concurrence.

3. PUBLICATION TIMELINE (standard)
   - Provisional result: within 3 weeks of the last
     examination of the semester.
   - Final result: after the moderation committee signs off
     and, for graduating students, after the final
     clearance (degree_requirements.txt).
   - Results are published on the student portal and the
     notice board of the Controller of Examinations.

4. RE-TOTALING AND REVIEW
   - A student may apply for re-totaling (arithmetic check of
     the final script) within 15 days of result publication,
     with the prescribed fee (Section I).
   - The Controller re-totals in presence of the department's
     nominee; the outcome is final for re-totaling.
   - A full script review (beyond arithmetic) is not
     available; grade change for clerical error only
     (grading_policy.txt §3).

5. INCOMPLETE (I) GRADES IN RESULTS
   Courses graded "I" are excluded from SGPA/CGPA until
   cleared; after the supplementary/makeup examination the
   grade is entered and GPA recomputed (see grading_policy.txt
   §2 for the deadline).

6. RESULT WITHHOLDING
   Results may be withheld for dues (Section I) or pending
   disciplinary cases (Section J); withheld results are
   released upon clearance, not re-computed.

7. CORRECTION OF RECORDS
   Errors in a published result (name, ID, course list) are
   corrected by the Registrar's Office (Section A) on
   production of documents; a corrected transcript is issued
   per Section H procedures.
