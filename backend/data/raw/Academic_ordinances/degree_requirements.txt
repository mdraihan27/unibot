DEGREE REQUIREMENTS AND AWARD
=============================

1. REQUIREMENTS FOR AWARD (clause 10.2)
   To be awarded the degree, a student must:
   a. Complete ALL required courses of the approved curriculum
      (Section G) with passing grades;
   b. Earn the minimum total programme credits:
        B.Sc. Engg.              160
        B.Sc. (Hons)/B.Pharm     per approved curriculum
                                 (150–160)
        BBA                      per approved curriculum
                                 (130–140)
        B.A. (Hons) English      130
        DVM                      per DVM schedule (200+)
   c. Achieve CGPA of 2.00 or above;
   d. Complete thesis/project and internship requirements
      where the curriculum requires them (thesis_project
      _guidelines.txt, internship_guidelines.txt);
   e. Clear ALL dues (Section I) and return all university/
      hall property (Section F for hall items, Section A —
      Librarian for library items);
   f. Have no pending disciplinary case (Section J).

2. FINAL CLEARANCE PROCEDURE
   Step   Office / Action
   ------------------------------------------------------------
   1      Apply to the Chairman for "final clearance" in the
          final semester after the last final examination.
   2      Department verifies curriculum completion.
   3      Hall Provost issues hall clearance (Section F).
   4      Librarian issues no-dues certificate (Section A).
   5      Accounts Office issues financial clearance
          (Section A — Director of Accounts; Section I for
          fee settlement).
   6      Controller of Examinations verifies results and
          forwards the graduand list to the Academic Council.
   7      Registrar issues the provisional certificate and
          arranges convocation.

3. AWARD AND RECORD
   - Degrees are awarded at convocation; a provisional
     certificate is issued earlier through the certificate
     procedures in Section H.
   - The transcript shows the CGPA, honours classification
     (cgpa_computation.txt §7), and all courses including
     repeats, improvements, and transfers.
   - The original degree certificate is issued once; duplicate
     procedures are in Section H.

4. POSTHUMOUS / SPECIAL CASES
   Degrees in absentia or posthumous awards follow Registrar's
   Office rules under the University Act (Section A).
