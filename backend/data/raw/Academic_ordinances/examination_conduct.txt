EXAMINATION CONDUCT RULES AND MALPRACTICE
=========================================

1. EXAMINATION ENTRY
   - Admit card: issued by the Controller of Examinations via
     the student portal after dues clearance (Section I).
   - Student ID card must be shown at the room door; without
     both, entry is refused.
   - Seating is per the published seat plan; students must sit
     in the assigned seat.

2. PERMITTED AND PROHIBITED MATERIALS
   Permitted: pens, pencils, ruler, approved calculator (for
     courses where announced), geometry box.
   Prohibited: mobile phones/smart devices of any kind, smart
     watches, notes, books, programmable devices unless
     announced, any communication with another candidate.
   Any prohibited item found, whether used or not, is
   malpractice.

3. CONDUCT DURING THE EXAMINATION
   - No entry after 30 minutes from the start; no exit during
     the first 60 minutes or the last 15 minutes.
   - Scripts must be signed on the attendance sheet.
   - Raising papers, looking at another's script, or passing
     materials constitutes malpractice.
   - Invigilators' instructions are binding; arguing with an
     invigilator is itself a reportable offense.

4. MALPRACTICE — PENALTIES (clause 6.9)
   Finding                                   Penalty
   ------------------------------------------------------------
   Possession of prohibited material        F in the course;
                                            disciplinary case
   Copying / communication                   F in the course +
                                            suspension per
                                            Section J
   Impersonation (sitting for another)       F + expulsion
                                            (Section J)
   Forging signature / document              Expulsion
   Leaking question paper                    Expulsion + legal
                                            referral

   The malpractice committee (chaired by the Proctor, with a
   nominee of the department and a Controller of Examinations
   representative) hears the case and recommends penalties;
   the Vice-Chancellor approves. The full disciplinary
   process and appeal route is in Section J.

5. ILLNESS DURING EXAMINATION
   A student taken ill mid-examination leaves under
   invigilator supervision and may apply for an Incomplete
   (grading_policy.txt §2) with the Medical Centre certificate.

6. EXAMINATION SCHEDULE CONFLICTS
   Two examinations scheduled at the same time are resolved by
   the Controller of Examinations, which reschedules one to a
   makeup slot within the examination period.
