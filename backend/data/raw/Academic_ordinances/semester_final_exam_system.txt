SEMESTER FINAL EXAMINATION SYSTEM
=================================

1. AUTHORITY AND SCHEDULING
   - Final examinations are conducted by the Office of the
     Controller of Examinations (Section A) under rules
     approved by the Academic Council.
   - The examination routine (date, time, room per course) is
     published at least 10 days before the first examination
     and is also in the academic calendar (Section K).
   - A course must have completed its syllabus to be
     examined; courses behind schedule are flagged by the
     department and the examination may be deferred by the
     Dean with the Controller informed.

2. EXAMINATION FORMAT
   - Question papers are set by the course teacher, moderated
     internally, and approved by an external examiner/expert
     where the Ordinance requires external moderation (final-
     year and capstone courses).
   - Papers are printed and sealed by the Controller's office;
     custody is logged.

3. SEAT PLANS AND INVIGILATION
   - Seat plans are published with the routine and posted at
     each room door.
   - Invigilators are appointed from faculty lists; each room
     has at least one invigilator per 30 candidates.
   - The Proctorial body (Section A — Office of the Proctor)
     patrols examination blocks.

4. SCRIPT MANAGEMENT
   - Scripts are counted in the room, sealed per session, and
     logged into the Controller's custody the same day.
   - Scripts are retained for one academic year after result
     publication, then disposed of per record rules.

5. MAKEUP / DEFERRED EXAMINATIONS
   - A student barred from an examination for attendance
     (attendance_policy.txt) is NOT eligible for makeup; the
     course is repeated.
   - A student with an approved Incomplete (illness during the
     exam, grading_policy.txt §2) sits the supplementary
     examination per supplementary_exams.txt.

6. CENTRAL CONTROL ROOM
   A central control room operates during the examination
   period for lost admit cards, seat plan disputes, and
   emergency communications: +880-421-61300 (synthetic).

7. POST-EXAMINATION
   Marking is completed within 7 days; tabulation follows
   result_publication.txt.
