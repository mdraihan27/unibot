LEAVE OF ABSENCE, WITHDRAWAL AND READMISSION
============================================

1. TYPES OF LEAVE (clause 7.5)
   Leave Type            Max Duration      Approval
   ------------------------------------------------------------
   Semester leave        2 consecutive     Chairman + Dean
   (personal)            semesters
   Medical leave         Up to 2 years     Chairman + Dean +
                         total with Medical  Medical Centre
                         Centre certificate  certification
   Maternity leave       Per statute       Dean (Registrar
                                           notified)
   Mandatory duty        Course of duty   VC, on Proctor's
   (sports/service)                     recommendation

2. APPLICATION PROCEDURE
   a. Apply in writing to the Chairman with supporting
      documents BEFORE the leave period begins (retroactive
      leave is granted only for hospitalization).
   b. Clearance from the Hall Provost (Section F) and the
      department is required for the leave to be recorded.
   c. Leave is recorded on the transcript as "Leave —
      Semester X" with no GPA effect.

3. EFFECT ON STANDING
   - Leave semesters do not count as probation semesters.
   - The maximum-duration clock (probation_suspension
     _termination.txt §5) PAUSES during approved leave and
     resumes on return.

4. WITHDRAWAL FROM THE UNIVERSITY
   A student withdrawing entirely applies to the Registrar
   (Section A) through the Chairman; a withdrawal record is
   kept and the student may apply for readmission within the
   maximum duration, subject to seat availability and the
   prevailing curriculum.

5. READMISSION
   - After suspension: see probation_suspension_termination
     .txt §3.
   - After withdrawal/leave beyond two semesters: reapply
     through the Registrar's office; credit for completed
     courses is retained per the prevailing curriculum
     equivalence list issued by the department.

6. FEE DURING LEAVE
   Semester fees are not charged for fully approved leave
   semesters; partial-semester leave follows the refund rules
   in Section I.
