THESIS AND PROJECT GUIDELINES
=============================

1. SCOPE
   Final-year thesis/project (codes XY90–99, 4–6 credits) is
   required in most 4-year programmes; exact credit is per the
   approved curriculum (Section G).

2. SUPERVISOR ASSIGNMENT
   - Each student (or group, where permitted) is assigned a
     supervisor from the department faculty (Section D rosters)
     in the semester before the thesis semester begins.
   - A supervisor supervises at most 8 thesis students per
     session.
   - Co-supervision from another department or an external
     industry expert requires the Chairman's approval.

3. STAGES AND DEADLINES (standard schedule)
   Stage                    When (relative to semester)
   ------------------------------------------------------------
   Topic approval + proposal  Weeks 1–3
   Midterm progress review    Week 8 (written progress report)
   Pre-submission review      Week 13
   Submission of report       End of week 15
   Presentation + viva        Final examination period

4. ASSESSMENT (100 marks, rubric-based)
   Component                 Marks
   ------------------------------------------------------------
   Supervisor's evaluation   30
   Report (external/internal
   examiner)                 30
   Presentation + viva voce  30
   Midterm progress          10
   ------------------------------------------------------------
   A second examiner (internal, not the supervisor) and an
   external examiner (for final-year projects) moderate the
   report and presentation.

5. INTEGRITY
   - Plagiarism check: reports are screened with the
     university's similarity tool; similarity above 20%
     (excluding references and standard definitions) requires
     revision before acceptance; above 40% is referred as
     malpractice (examination_conduct.txt §4 applies with the
     Proctor's committee).
   - Data fabrication is malpractice.
   - Group work must state each member's contribution; the
     viva examines individual understanding.

6. PUBLICATION / DISSEMINATION
   Departments may require a presentation in the departmental
     seminar series; best theses may be nominated for
     university-level awards per the Research Cell's notice
     (Section A).
