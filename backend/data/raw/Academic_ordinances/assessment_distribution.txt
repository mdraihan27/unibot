ASSESSMENT AND MARKS DISTRIBUTION
=================================

1. CURRENT SYSTEM (Amendment II — effective from session
   2015-16; clause 6.2)
   Every credit-bearing course is assessed in two parts:

   Component               Marks     Nature
   ------------------------------------------------------------
   Incourse (continuous)   40        Class tests, tutorials,
                                     assignments, attendance
   Final examination       60        Semester-end examination
   ------------------------------------------------------------
   Total                   100

2. INCOURSE BREAKDOWN (standard default)
   Component                         Marks (of the 40)
   ------------------------------------------------------------
   Class tests / midterm assessments   20  (best 2 of 3 tests)
   Tutorial / assignments / quizzes    10
   Attendance                          10  (see §4 below)

   A department may redistribute within the 40-mark incourse
   block per its approved assessment policy filed with the
   Controller of Examinations, but the total must remain 40.

3. FINAL EXAMINATION (60 marks)
   - Conducted centrally by the Controller of Examinations
     (Section A) per the published routine (Section K).
   - Duration: typically 3 hours for a 3-credit theory course;
     shorter for 1–2 credit courses.
   - Questions cover the full syllabus; at least 20% of marks
     come from the portion after the last class test unless
     the department files a different split.

4. ATTENDANCE MARKS (within the 10-mark attendance component)
   Attendance %           Marks (of 10)
   ------------------------------------------------------------
   90% and above          10
   85–89%                  9
   80–84%                  8
   75–79%                  7
   below 75%              0  — AND the student is barred from
                                 the final exam (see
                                 attendance_policy.txt)

5. PRACTICAL / LABORATORY COURSES
   Assessed by continuous lab evaluation (quizzes, reports,
   final lab test) totaling 100, split per the department's
   filed assessment policy; no separate central final
   examination unless the course is coded as a theory course
   with a lab component.

6. THESIS / PROJECT / INTERNSHIP
   Assessed per thesis_project_guidelines.txt and
   internship_guidelines.txt (rubric-based, panel evaluation).

7. SUPPRESSED COMPONENT
   Class tests missed for valid documented reasons are excused
   and the remaining tests re-weighted; undocumented absences
   score zero on the missed test. There is no makeup class
   test after the final examination schedule is published.

================================================================
PRE-2015-16 SYSTEM (SUPERSEDED — ARCHIVE ONLY)
================================================================
   Under the original ordinance, each course carried a single
   final examination of 100 marks with 25% sessional/internal
   assessment. This system applied to students admitted before
   the 2015-16 session and is retained only for their records.
   It does NOT apply to current students.
