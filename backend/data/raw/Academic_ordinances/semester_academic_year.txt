SEMESTER SYSTEM AND ACADEMIC YEAR
=================================

1. DEFINITIONS
   Semester: a fixed instructional term of the academic year.
   Academic Year: two regular semesters (Spring and Fall)
   plus, for most programmes, an optional Summer term.

2. STRUCTURE
   Term          Typical Months (varies per academic calendar)
   ----------------------------------------------------------------
   Spring        January – May     (classes ~15 weeks + exams)
   Summer        June – July       (optional, ~8 weeks; limited
                                  course offering)
   Fall          August – December (classes ~15 weeks + exams)

   The specific start/end dates for each session are published
   annually in the Academic Calendar (Section K) and approved
   by the Academic Council.

3. SEMESTER COMPONENTS (per clause 3.2)
   a. Classes: approximately 15 teaching weeks in a regular
      semester (8 weeks in Summer).
   b. Mid-semester break: none; continuous instruction.
   c. Final examinations: approximately 3 weeks, scheduled
      after classes end.
   d. Result processing and publication: approximately 3-4
      weeks after the last examination (see
      result_publication.txt).

4. CREDIT LOAD PER SEMESTER
   - Normal load: 15–19 credit hours per regular semester.
   - Overload (up to 21 credit hours, or 22 for graduating
     seniors with Dean's permission): requires a CGPA of 3.50
     or above and written approval of the Chairman and Dean.
   - Underload (below 12 credit hours): only in the final
     semester, or with a Dean-approved reduced load for
     documented reasons (see leave_of_absence.txt).

5. COURSE OFFERING SEQUENCE
   Courses are offered in the semester(s) indicated in the
   approved curriculum of each department (Section G). A
   course offered in the "wrong" semester may be taken only
   with the Chairman's written permission, and prerequisite
   rules (see course_registration.txt) still apply.

6. SUMMER TERM RULES
   - Open to all students; intended for backlog (F-graded or
     incomplete) courses and accelerated progress.
   - Maximum 9 credit hours.
   - Not all courses are offered each Summer; the offering
     list is published by the department before registration.
   - Fees for Summer courses are charged per credit hour
     (Section I).
