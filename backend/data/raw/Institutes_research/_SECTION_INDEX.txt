ACCESS MODEL NOTE
================================================================
Four of these five units follow a "route through your Department
Chairman or supervisor" access model — they are primarily research
infrastructure, not student-facing service points. CLSD is the
exception: it takes direct student registration, similar to how the
Office of Student Counselling and Guidance (Section A) works. A RAG
system should not treat all five units as equally accessible to an
individual student asking "how do I use X" — CLSD gets a direct
answer; the other four get a "talk to your supervisor/Chairman
first" answer.
