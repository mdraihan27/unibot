COMPLAINT COMMITTEE
================================================================

1. PURPOSE
   The Complaint Committee is the University's general grievance
   redress body for complaints not otherwise routed to a specialized
   committee (e.g., the Sexual Harassment Complaint Committee, which
   operates separately — see sexual_harassment_policy.txt in
   Section J once generated).

3. SCOPE OF COMPLAINTS HANDLED
   - Administrative service failures (delays, non-response from an
     office beyond the Citizen Charter commitment)
   - Academic grievances not covered by a specific appeal process
     (e.g., grade disputes not resolved at department level)
   - General staff/faculty conduct complaints not involving
     harassment or ragging (which have dedicated committees)
   - Facilities and service quality complaints

4. HOW TO FILE A COMPLAINT
   Step 1: Submit in writing via email (complaints@just.edu.bd) or
           in person at the Registrar's Office, Room 105.
   Step 2: Include: your name, registration/employee ID, the office
           or individual concerned, a factual description of the
           issue, and any prior attempts to resolve it directly.
   Step 3: Acknowledgement is sent within 3 working days.
   Step 4: The Committee reviews and may request additional
           information or a meeting with the complainant.
   Step 5: A resolution or status update is provided within 30
           working days of the original submission.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY
   Complaints are handled with reasonable confidentiality; the
   respondent (person/office complained about) will typically be
   informed of the complaint's substance as part of due process,
   but the complainant's identity is not broadly publicized.

6. ESCALATION
   If unsatisfied with the Committee's resolution, a complainant may
   appeal in writing to the Vice-Chancellor within 15 days of the
   Committee's decision.

7. RELATIONSHIP TO OTHER COMMITTEES
   - Ragging complaints: routed to the Proctor's Office /
     Anti-Ragging Committee (faster response time, see
     anti_ragging_policy.txt).
   - Sexual harassment complaints: routed to the dedicated Sexual
     Harassment Complaint Committee (see sexual_harassment_policy.txt),
     NOT this general Complaint Committee.
   - Examination malpractice: routed to the Examination Committee
     and Controller of Examinations, not this Committee.
