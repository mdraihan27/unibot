1. ESTABLISHMENT
   JUST is established as an autonomous public university by Act of
   Parliament (synthetic reference: "Jashore University of Science
   and Technology Act, 2007").

2. CORPORATE STATUS
   The University is a body corporate with perpetual succession, a
   common seal, and the capacity to sue and be sued, acquire and hold
   property, in its own name (standard public university provisions).

3. STATUTORY BODIES ESTABLISHED BY THE ACT
   - Regent Board
   - Academic Council
   - Finance Committee
   - Selection Boards (for faculty recruitment)

4. STATUTES AND ORDINANCES
   - "Statutes" govern the University's basic structure, officer
     appointments, and body compositions; amendment requires Regent
     Board approval and, in some cases, government assent.
   - "Ordinances" govern academic and examination matters in detail
     (see academic_ordinance files); approved by Academic Council and
     ratified by Regent Board.
   - "Regulations" govern day-to-day administrative procedures; may
     be approved at Registrar/VC level without full Regent Board
     process, depending on scope.

5. REGULATORY OVERSIGHT
   JUST operates under the oversight of the University Grants
   Commission (UGC) of Bangladesh for quality assurance, funding
   coordination, and inter-university policy alignment. See
   http://www.ugc.gov.bd/ (real external link referenced by JUST's
   site).
