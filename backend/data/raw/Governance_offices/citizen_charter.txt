CITIZEN CHARTER (SYNTHETIC)
================================================================
A Citizen Charter is a public commitment by a government institution
on the services it provides, to whom, and within what timeframe.
Below is a synthetic version modeling the expected structure.

SERVICE: Admission Information
  Provided by: Admissions Office / Central Admission Committee
  Response time commitment: Admission circular published minimum 60
    days before test date; queries answered within 3 working days.

SERVICE: Transcript Issuance
  Provided by: Office of the Controller of Examinations
  Response time commitment: 7-10 working days standard; 3 working
    days for urgent requests (fee applies).

SERVICE: Certificate Issuance (Provisional/Final/Duplicate)
  Provided by: Office of the Controller of Examinations
  Response time commitment: 10-15 working days.

SERVICE: Fee Payment Support
  Provided by: Office of the Director of Accounts
  Response time commitment: Same-day receipt confirmation for online
    payments; 2 working days for bank-branch payment reconciliation.

SERVICE: Complaint Redress (General)
  Provided by: Complaint Committee (see complaint_committee.txt)
  Response time commitment: Acknowledgement within 3 working days;
    resolution or status update within 30 working days.

SERVICE: Hall Seat Allocation
  Provided by: Respective Hall Provost Office
  Response time commitment: Allocation list published within 15
    working days of application deadline.

SERVICE: ID Card Issuance/Reissue
  Provided by: Office of the Registrar (Academic Branch) / ICT Cell
  Response time commitment: 5 working days.

GRIEVANCE ESCALATION
  If a service commitment above is not met, the citizen may escalate
  in writing to the Registrar's Office, referencing this Charter.
