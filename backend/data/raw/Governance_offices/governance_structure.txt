GOVERNANCE STRUCTURE OF JUST
================================================================

LEVEL 1 — CEREMONIAL HEAD
  Chancellor (ex officio, Head of State of Bangladesh per University
  Act). Presides over Convocation; confers degrees. Not part of daily
  administration. See https://bangabhaban.gov.bd/ (external, real link
  referenced by JUST's official site for context only).

LEVEL 2 — CHIEF EXECUTIVE
  Vice-Chancellor (VC) — chief executive and academic officer.
  Appointed per the University Act. Chairs the Regent Board and
  Academic Council. See office_vice_chancellor.txt.

  Pro-Vice-Chancellor (Pro-VC) — assists the VC; delegated authority
  over academic quality, cross-faculty coordination. See
  office_pro_vice_chancellor.txt.

  Treasurer — chief financial authority. Chairs the Finance
  Committee. See office_treasurer.txt.

LEVEL 3 — GOVERNING BODIES
  Regent Board — highest governing body. Approves budget, ordinances,
  senior appointments, new faculties/departments. Meets minimum twice
  yearly.

  Academic Council — highest academic body. Approves curricula,
  admission policy, examination regulations. Composition: VC (Chair),
  Pro-VC, all Deans, all Department Chairmen, Controller of
  Examinations, Chief Librarian, Registrar (Secretary).

  Syndicate/Finance Committee — financial oversight subordinate to
  Regent Board, chaired by Treasurer.

LEVEL 4 — FACULTY LEADERSHIP
  Deans — one per Faculty (8 total), appointed by VC from senior
  professors, typically 2-year rotating terms. See faculty files in
  Section C.

LEVEL 5 — DEPARTMENT LEADERSHIP
  Department Chairmen — one per Department (26 total), typically
  2-year rotating terms among eligible senior faculty. See
  department files in Section D.

LEVEL 6 — ADMINISTRATIVE OFFICES
  Registrar, Controller of Examinations, Proctor, Chief Librarian,
  Director of Accounts, Chief Engineer, Chief Medical Officer, and
  other office heads — see individual office files in this section.
  These report administratively to the VC (directly or via the
  Registrar) and operationally support both governance bodies and
  day-to-day student/staff services.

================================================================
DECISION-MAKING FLOW (SIMPLIFIED)
================================================================
  New Course/Curriculum Change:
    Department Academic Committee -> Faculty -> Academic Council ->
    (if credit/ordinance impact) Regent Board

  Disciplinary Case (serious):
    Complaint -> Proctor's Office -> Disciplinary Committee ->
    Vice-Chancellor -> (if expulsion) Regent Board ratification

  Budget Approval:
    Department/Office request -> Director of Accounts -> Treasurer ->
    Finance Committee -> Regent Board

  Examination Result Publication:
    Examination Committee -> Controller of Examinations ->
    Vice-Chancellor approval -> Publication
