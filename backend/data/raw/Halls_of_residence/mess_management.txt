MESS MANAGEMENT AND DINING RULES
================================

1. MESS COMMITTEE
   Each hall has an elected Mess Committee:
     - President: Provost (ex officio)
     - Secretary: one elected resident
     - Members: one representative per block + the Hall
       Accounts Assistant (ex officio, keeps the books)
   The Committee meets monthly to review accounts, menu, and
   the meal rate. Minutes are posted on the hall notice board.

2. MEAL RATE (synthetic standing rates)
   - Monthly advance ( mess deposit ): BDT 3,000 at seat
     allotment, adjusted against actuals monthly.
   - Running monthly bill: BDT 95–120 per day depending on
     menu cycle, billed in advance on the 25th of the
     previous month.
   - Guests: meals at BDT 120 per meal (breakfast BDT 50) on
     a guest meal card purchased from the Hall Office;
     residents may not give their meal card to others.
   Rates are reviewed by the Mess Committee each semester;
   changes require the Provost's approval and are posted
   14 days in advance. Fee payment procedures: Section I.

3. MEAL TIMINGS (standard)
   Breakfast   7:30 - 9:00 AM
   Lunch       1:00 - 2:30 PM
   Dinner      8:00 - 9:30 PM
   (Ramadan schedule announced separately by the Committee.)

4. MEAL CARD / TOKEN RULES
   - Meals are served against a token collected at the dining
     hall entrance; tokens are issued per meal against the
     monthly bill.
   - A resident absent for 3+ consecutive days may apply for
     rebate of board charges with the Secretary's
     certification; rebate applies from the 4th day.
   - Food cannot be taken out of the dining hall except for
     documented medical need (Medical Centre note, Section A).

5. KITCHEN HYGIENE AND DIETARY NEEDS
   - The University Medical Centre (Section A) certifies
     special dietary requirements; the mess accommodates
     medical diets at no extra charge.
   - Water quality is tested monthly by the Estate Office
     (Section A — Chief Engineer); reports are posted.
   - Food complaints go to the Mess Secretary first; unresolved
     complaints go to the Assistant Provost (duty).

6. MESS ACCOUNT AUDIT
   Mess accounts are audited each semester by the Hall
   Accounts Assistant and countersigned by the Provost;
   the Treasurer's office (Section A) audits hall finances
   annually.
