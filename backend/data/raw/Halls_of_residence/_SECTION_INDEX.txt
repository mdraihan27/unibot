================================================================
SECTION F — HALLS OF RESIDENCE
================================================================

SECTION SCOPE
  Section F covers residential life: the halls of residence,
  their administration (Provosts, Assistant Provosts, House
  Tutors), room allocation, admission to halls, mess
  operation, hall rules, and clearance procedures.

  Section A index reference: "Halls of Residence / Provost
  details (Section F)".

================================================================
WHAT'S NOT IN THIS SECTION
================================================================
  - Hall fees and payment: Section I (fees and payment
    procedures) — hall fee amounts are referenced from there
  - Disciplinary offences and the formal hearing process:
    Section J — hall offences are REPORTED here, HEARD under
    Section J with the Proctor's office (Section A)
  - Guest house / visitor accommodation for outsiders:
    handled by the Physical Education / guest house under the
    Registrar's office (Section A) — hall guest rooms are for
    resident students' families only
  - Medical services: Section A (Chief Medical Officer);
    medical leave interaction with hall residence:
    leave_of_absence.txt (Section E)

================================================================
DATA GENERATION METHOD NOTE
================================================================
Hall names follow the naming pattern of the real JUST campus
as described by the user (the real university's actual hall
names should replace these if the user supplies them). All
provost names, staff, phone numbers, capacities, and room
counts are synthetically generated for RAG testing only.
