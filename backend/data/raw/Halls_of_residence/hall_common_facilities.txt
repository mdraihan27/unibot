HALL COMMON FACILITIES
======================

1. STANDARD FACILITIES (all six halls)
   Facility              Details
   ------------------------------------------------------------
   Dining hall           Seating for ~40% of residents per
                         sitting; two sittings where needed
   Common room           TV, newspapers, carrom/chess boards;
                         booking via Common Room Committee
   Reading room          Quiet study, 8 AM - 11 PM
   Prayer room           Separate facilities for male/female
                         residents per hall layout
   Guest room            2 rooms per hall; family guests of
                         residents only; BDT 300/night
                         (Section I), booked via Hall Office
   Laundry yard          Washing lines / machines (where
                         installed), self-service
   Water & power         24-hour supply; corridor lighting
                         on motion sensors
   Wi-Fi                 Campus Wi-Fi (Section A — ICT Cell)
                         coverage in all halls; resident
                         devices registered by MAC address

2. SPORTS AND RECREATION
   - Volleyball/badminton court adjacent to each hall cluster
   - Indoor games equipment issued from the hall office
     against student ID card
   - Inter-hall tournaments are organized by the Office of
     Physical Education (Section A) each Spring semester

3. HEALTH AND SAFETY
   - First-aid boxes at each hall office; duty Assistant
     Provost is the first contact for resident medical
     emergencies, with the Medical Centre (Section A) on
     call
   - Fire extinguishers per block corridor; evacuation drill
     once per year organized with the Proctor's office
     (Section A)
   - Canteens inside hall compounds operate under the Mess
     Committee license and close at 11 PM

4. MAINTENANCE REQUESTS
   Fault reports (plumbing, electrical, furniture) are lodged
   at the hall office on the register or by phone; routine
   repairs within 3 working days, emergency repairs same day.
   Repeated unreported damage becomes a chargeable item at
   clearance (hall_clearance_procedure.txt).
