HALL ADMISSION RULES AND SEAT ALLOCATION
========================================

1. ELIGIBILITY
   a. Any enrolled student (Sections D/E) may apply for a
      hall seat; priority order:
        P1  First-year students from outside the district
        P2  Students with CGPA-based merit renewal (continuing)
        P3  Final-year students from outside the district
        P4  Other students (district residents, local)
   b. A student with a pending disciplinary case (Section J)
      or unpaid hall dues (Section I) is NOT eligible for
      admission or renewal.
   c. Married students: single seats only; family
      accommodation is not provided in undergraduate halls.

2. APPLICATION WINDOW
   - New students: with the admission/enrolment process;
      seats are allocated before classes begin. See the
      admission notice (Section K) for dates.
   - Continuing students: a renewal application each year
      before the start of the Fall semester.
   - Vacant seats mid-year: published by the hall office;
      applications within 7 days of the notice.

3. ALLOCATION PROCEDURE
   Step   Actor        Action
   ------------------------------------------------------------
   1      Student      Submit application (online portal +
                      signed paper form to the hall office)
   2      Hall office  Verify eligibility (dues, discipline,
                      distance from home district)
   3      Provost      Approve list per priority order;
                      publish merit/waiting list
   4      Student      Pay seat rent + advance mess deposit
                      (Section I) within 5 working days
   5      Hall office  Issue seat allotment order and room key

4. WAITING LIST
   - Published with the merit list; seats vacated within the
     semester are offered to the waiting list in order.
   - A waiting-list offer lapses after 3 working days if not
     accepted with payment.

5. SEAT RENEWAL
   - Renewable annually; renewal requires satisfactory
     hall conduct (no unresolved hall offence in the
     preceding year) and CGPA of 2.00 or above (standing
     rules, Section E — probation_suspension_termination
     .txt).
   - The Provost may withhold renewal for repeated hall-rule
     violations; the student may appeal to the Proctor
     (Section A) within 10 working days.

6. VACATION OF SEAT
   A seat is vacated by: graduation, withdrawal/termination
   (Section E), leave of more than one semester
   (leave_of_absence.txt — the seat is NOT held), or
   expulsion (Section J). Clearance is per
   hall_clearance_procedure.txt.
