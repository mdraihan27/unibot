HALL RULES AND REGULATIONS
==========================

1. GENERAL CONDUCT
   - Residents are subject to ALL University rules (Section E
     academic rules; Section J disciplinary code) plus these
     hall rules. A hall offence may also be a University
     offence; the Provost reports University offences to the
     Proctor (Section A).
   - The student ID card must be shown at the hall gate on
     entry after 8 PM.
   - Ragging is absolutely prohibited; see Section J for the
     definition, reporting route, and penalties.

2. VISITORS
   - Visitors (non-resident students): lobby sitting area
     only, 9 AM - 8 PM, entry against own ID card in the
     gate register.
   - Visitors of the opposite gender: lobby/common room of
     the designated visitor area only; entry 9 AM - 8 PM;
     never in living blocks, dining hall, or prayer rooms.
   - Overnight guests: only in the hall guest room booked per
     hall_common_facilities.txt; residents may not host
     overnight guests in rooms.

3. QUIET HOURS AND CURFEW
   - Quiet hours: 8 PM - 8 AM for study; loud music/instruments
     prohibited outside common rooms at all times.
   - Entry curfew: residents enter by 10 PM; the gate register
     records late entry, which the Provost reviews weekly.
   - Repeated late entry (3+ in a month) is reported to
     guardians and becomes a hall offence.

4. PROHIBITED IN HALL ROOMS
   - Cooking appliances (except electric kettle where the hall
     permits), room heaters
   - Firearms, knives, explosives of any kind
   - Illegal substances (alcohol, narcotics) — Section J
     penalties apply
   - Pets

5. ROOM INVENTORY AND CARE
   - Each seat comes with an inventory (bed, mattress, desk,
     chair, bookshelf, fan, light) signed for at allotment.
   - Inter-room furniture transfer is prohibited and treated
     as theft/damage at clearance.
   - Room painting, fixtures, or electrical modification
     require the Caretaker's written approval.

6. ELECTED BODIES AND MEETINGS
   - Hall Union / Residents' Committee elections each
     semester under the supervision of the Provost.
   - General meetings require the Provost's prior approval;
     political party activity inside the hall compound is
     prohibited.

7. REPORTING ROUTES
   Issue                    First contact        Then
   ------------------------------------------------------------
   Room/mess complaint      Duty Asst. Provost  Provost
   Security incident        Security desk 24 hr Proctor
   Ragging/harassment       Provost (any hour)  Section J
   Medical emergency        Duty Asst. Provost  Medical Ctr.
   Electric/water fault     Hall office         Chief Engineer
