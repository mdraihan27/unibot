ROOM AND SEAT ALLOCATION POLICY
===============================

1. ROOM TYPES
   Hall          Single rooms   Double rooms   Triple rooms
   ------------------------------------------------------------
   Hall 1 (MRH)      -             50%            50%
   Hall 2 (LSH)      -             60%            40%
   Hall 3 (SHH)      10%           70%            20%
   Hall 4 (BKZH)     10%           70%            20%
   Hall 5 (JSH)      60%           40%             -
   Hall 6 (NRH)      20%           80%             -
   (percentages approximate; exact room counts at the hall
   office)

2. FIRST-YEAR ASSIGNMENT
   - First-year students are allotted DOUBLE/TRIPLE rooms,
     normally with a roommate from a different department
     within the same faculty to support the residential
     tutorial programme (Office of Student Counselling,
     Section A).
   - Room changes in the first semester are allowed once,
     with the mutual written consent of both roommates and
     the Assistant Provost.

3. SINGLE ROOMS
   - Priority: (1) medical need certified by the Medical
     Centre, (2) final-year students with CGPA 3.50+,
     (3) postgraduate students (Hall 5), (4) seniority.
   - Single rooms carry a higher seat rent (Section I).

4. CHANGE OF ROOM
   - Mid-year changes only for documented reasons (medical,
     conflict resolved by counselling) approved by the
     Provost.
   - Room swap between two residents requires both to apply
     together.

5. VACANT BEDS
   - A bed left vacant for 15+ consecutive days without
     intimation may be re-allotted; the absent resident's
     belongings are packed by the office and stored for
     collection within 30 days.
   - Semester leave (leave_of_absence.txt, Section E): the
     seat is NOT held; re-application on return per
     hall_admission_rules.txt.

6. ROOM INSPECTION
   - The Provost/Assistant Provosts may inspect rooms with
     24-hour notice (without notice for suspected safety
     hazard or reported offence, with a second staff member
     present).
