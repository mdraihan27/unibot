HALL CLEARANCE PROCEDURE
========================

1. WHEN CLEARANCE IS REQUIRED
   - Graduation (part of final clearance, degree_requirements
     .txt, Section E)
   - Withdrawal, suspension, or termination (Sections E/J)
   - Leave of one semester or more (the seat is vacated —
     leave_of_absence.txt)
   - Inter-hall transfer (rare; Provost-to-Provost memo)

2. CLEARANCE STEPS
   Step   Where              What is checked
   ------------------------------------------------------------
   1      Room / Block       Furniture inventory complete
                             and undamaged; personal items
                             removed
   2      Mess office        Mess bill settled to date;
                             meal card / token returned
   3      Hall office        Keys + ID-related hall items
                             returned; no pending hall
                             offence
   4      Accounts           Advance deposit adjusted;
                             damage charges (if any) paid
                             (Section I payment route)
   5      Provost            Signs the Hall Clearance
                             Certificate

3. DAMAGE CHARGES (synthetic standard rates)
   Item                    Charge (BDT)
   ------------------------------------------------------------
   Mattress                2,500
   Wooden bed frame        3,500
   Study desk              2,000
   Chair                   800
   Bookshelf               1,200
   Fan / light fitting     1,500 (+ installation)
   Room repainting (if     1,000
   unauthorized painting)
   Fair wear and tear is not charged; the Accounts Assistant
   assesses jointly with the Caretaker, and the resident may
   appeal the assessment to the Provost within 5 working days.

4. TIMELINE
   - Final-semester students: complete clearance within 10
     working days of the last final examination.
   - Others: within 5 working days of vacating.
   - The signed clearance certificate is surrendered to the
     Registrar's office for degree processing or to the
     department for leave/withdrawal records.

5. FAILURE TO CLEAR
   Uncleared dues become a registration hold (Section E —
   course_registration.txt §6) and appear on the student's
     financial record (Section I); degrees and transcripts
     are withheld until cleared (Section H).
